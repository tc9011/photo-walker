分析你的拍摄习惯

Photo Walker 读取相册里每张照片保存的拍摄参数，统计你最常用的相机、镜头和焦段，以及拍照的时间和地点。

  • 器材排行：最常用的相机品牌、型号、镜头和焦段
  • 拍摄日历：每一年、每一天拍了多少照片
  • 照片地图：照片在哪里拍的，去过多少国家和地区

只在你的设备上读取，不会上传照片。支持 iPhone 和 iPad。

See How You Shoot

Photo Walker reads the shooting settings saved in your photos to find out which cameras, lenses and focal lengths you use most, and when and where you take photos.

  • Gear: your most used camera brands, cameras, lenses and focal lengths
  • Calendar: how many photos you took each year and each day
  • Map: where your photos were taken, and the countries and regions you've been to

Read on your device only, never uploaded. For iPhone and iPad.
