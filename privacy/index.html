隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日

Photo Walker（下称「本 App」）由个人开发者 tc9011 开发，用来统计你的照片里保存的拍摄参数。你的照片不会离开你的设备。

本 App 读取的信息

  • 在你允许访问照片后，本 App 读取照片里保存的信息：相机品牌和型号、镜头、焦距、光圈、快门速度、感光度、曝光补偿、拍摄时间和照片里保存的拍摄地点，用来计算统计和显示照片。
  • 这些信息只保存在你设备上的本地数据库里。
  • 为了挑选显示的照片，本 App 用设备上的模型给照片评分（Apple 的 Vision 框架和一个 Core ML 美学评分模型），评分在设备上完成。

我们不收集的信息

  • 我们不收集、不上传你的照片、拍摄参数或拍摄地点，也没有保存你的数据的服务器。
  • 本 App 不需要账号，没有广告，不跟踪你。
  • 本 App 不使用你设备的定位。地图上显示的位置来自照片里保存的拍摄地点。

第三方服务

地图：Mapbox

显示地图时，本 App 从 Mapbox 下载地图数据。Mapbox 的地图 SDK 会向 Mapbox 发送去标识化的使用和诊断数据，例如设备型号、系统版本和地图的加载情况，这些数据不关联到你本人。点按地图上的 ⓘ 按钮，可以在「Mapbox Telemetry」里关闭。详见 Mapbox 隐私政策。

地名：Apple

为了显示拍摄地点的地名，本 App 把拍摄地点的坐标发给 Apple 的地理编码服务（不发送照片），查到的地名保存在设备上。详见 Apple 隐私政策。

iCloud 照片

如果你打开「从 iCloud 读取拍摄参数」，系统会从 iCloud 下载原图来读取参数，下载由 Apple 的「照片」服务完成。

数据的保存和删除

  • 统计数据保存在你的设备上。在「设置」里点「重置数据」可以删除并重新读取；删除本 App 会删除全部数据。
  • 本 App 不会修改或删除你相册里的照片。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童的。

政策的变更

本政策有变化时，我们会更新本页面和生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Photo Walker (“the app”) is made by tc9011, an independent developer. It shows statistics about the shooting settings saved in your photos. Your photos never leave your device.

What the app reads

  • Once you allow access to your photos, the app reads the information saved in them: the camera make and model, the lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, exposure compensation, the date and the location saved in the photo. It uses them to compute the statistics and show the photos.
  • This information is only kept in a database on your device.
  • To pick the photos it shows, the app scores them with on-device models (Apple's Vision framework and a Core ML aesthetics model). The scoring happens on your device.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect or upload your photos, their settings or their locations, and we have no server that keeps your data.
  • The app has no account, no ads and doesn't track you.
  • The app doesn't use the location of your device. The map shows the locations saved in your photos.

Third-party services

Map: Mapbox

To show the map, the app downloads map data from Mapbox. The Mapbox map SDK sends de-identified usage and diagnostic data to Mapbox, like the device model, the system version and how the map loaded, which isn't linked to you. You can turn it off with the ⓘ button of the map, under “Mapbox Telemetry”. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy.

Place names: Apple

To show the names of the places where photos were taken, the app sends their coordinates (not the photos) to Apple's geocoding service, and keeps the names on your device. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

iCloud Photos

When you turn on “Read camera data from iCloud”, the system downloads the originals from iCloud to read their settings. Apple's Photos service does the download.

Keeping and deleting the data

  • The statistics are kept on your device. Reset Data in Settings deletes them and reads the photos again, deleting the app deletes all of its data.
  • The app never changes or deletes the photos in your library.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
