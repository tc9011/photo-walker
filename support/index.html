常见问题

为什么要访问照片？

统计数据来自照片里保存的拍摄参数。照片只在你的设备上读取，不会上传。

只允许访问部分照片可以吗？

可以，Photo Walker 只统计你选的照片。在「设置」的「部分照片」里可以选择更多照片，或者允许访问全部照片；首页的提示可以关掉。

为什么有的照片没有相机、镜头和参数？

  • 原图只在 iCloud 的照片，要在「设置」里打开「从 iCloud 读取拍摄参数」，下载原图后才能读到，每小时大约 1000 张。
  • 截图、从网上保存的图片和一些编辑后导出的照片，本身就没有保存这些信息。

可以不统计手机拍的照片吗？

可以。在「设置」里关掉「统计手机拍摄的照片」，就只统计相机拍的照片。

为什么地图上没有某些照片？

地图只显示保存了拍摄地点的照片。相机拍的照片通常要用相机厂商的 App 同步手机的位置，才会有拍摄地点。

新拍的照片要手动更新吗？

不用。打开 App、回到 App 时，以及 App 开着时相册有变化，都会自动读取新照片，只读有变化的部分。

更新照片要多久？

取决于照片的数量。iOS 26 起，你开始的更新切到别的 App 也会继续，进度显示在锁屏上。挑选配图用的评分在 App 打开时进行，夜里充电时也会给剩下的照片评分。

怎么添加小组件？

先打开 App 读完照片。在主屏幕上长按空白处，点左上角的「编辑」›「添加小组件」，选 Photo Walker：每日一张、最近拍的和拍摄日历，各有几种尺寸。锁定屏幕的小组件在锁定屏幕的「自定」里添加。小组件的浅色、深色在「设置」›「小组件外观」里选。

小组件多久更新一次？

打开 App 时马上更新。App 没打开时，小组件会在系统允许时（通常半小时左右）读取相册的变化，加上新拍的照片。

怎么分享照片？

查看照片时点右上角的分享；在照片列表里点「选择」，一次最多分享 9 张。首页封面、拍摄之最、焦段和拍摄日历也能分享。样式有展签、参数条、胶片和叠字，说明里写哪些信息可以自己选。图片在设备上生成，存到相册或者用系统的分享面板发出去。

年度回顾在哪里？

每年 12 月和第二年 1 月，首页封面下面和「设置」的实验室里会出现这一年的回顾，这一年要有至少 30 张照片。回顾可以存成图片或视频。

按时间补地点会改我的照片吗？

不会自动改。打开后，推算出的地点只在 App 里用。要写入相册，在「写入相册」里选好照片并确认，才会修改这些照片的地点，写入可以撤销。从电脑同步到手机的照片改不了。

怎么删除数据？

在「设置」里点「重置数据」，或者删除 App。相册里的照片不会被删除。

Questions

Why does the app need access to my photos?

The statistics come from the shooting settings saved in your photos. They are read on your device only, never uploaded.

Can I allow access to some photos only?

Yes, Photo Walker then counts the photos you selected. Selected Photos in Settings selects more photos or allows access to all of them, and the reminder on the Statistics tab can be closed.

Why do some photos have no camera, lens or settings?

  • Photos whose original is only in iCloud need “Read camera data from iCloud” turned on in Settings: the originals are downloaded to read them, about 1,000 photos an hour.
  • Screenshots, images saved from the web and some edited exports don't have this information.

Can I leave out the photos taken with my phone?

Yes. Turn off “Count Photos Taken with Phones” in Settings, only the photos of cameras are then counted.

Why are some photos missing from the map?

The map shows the photos that have a location saved in them. Photos from cameras usually get one from the app of the camera maker, which syncs the location of your phone.

Do I need to update for new photos?

No. New photos are read when you open the app, when you come back to it, and when your library changes while it is open. Only what changed is read.

How long does an update take?

It depends on the number of photos. From iOS 26, an update you start keeps going when you switch to another app, with its progress on the Lock Screen. The photos are scored to pick the ones to show while the app is open, and at night while the phone charges.

How do I add a widget?

Open the app once to read your photos. Then touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and pick Photo Walker: Photo of the Day, Recent Roll and Shooting Calendar, in several sizes. Lock Screen widgets are added when you customize the Lock Screen. Choose light or dark widgets in Settings › Widget Appearance.

How often do the widgets update?

Right away when you open the app. While the app is closed, the widgets read the changes of your library when the system lets them, usually about every half hour, and add the new photos.

How do I share photos?

Tap the share button at the top right of a photo, or Select in a list of photos to share up to 9 at once. The cover, Records, Focal Lengths and the calendar of the Statistics tab can be shared too. Pick a Label, Strip, Film or Overlay print and what its caption says. The images are made on your device, then saved to Photos or sent with the share sheet of the system.

Where is the year in review?

In December and January, below the cover of the Statistics tab and in the Lab of Settings, for a year with at least 30 photos. It can be saved as images or a video.

Does Places by Time change my photos?

Not by itself. The places it infers are only used in the app. To write them to your library, pick the photos in Write to Library and confirm: only then is the place of these photos changed, and you can undo it. Photos synced from a computer can't be changed.

How do I delete the data?

Tap Reset Data in Settings, or delete the app. The photos in your library are never deleted.
