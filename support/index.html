常见问题

为什么要访问照片？

统计数据来自照片里保存的拍摄参数。照片只在你的设备上读取，不会上传。

只允许访问部分照片可以吗？

可以，Photo Walker 只统计你选的照片。首页会提示「选择更多照片」，可以随时加。

为什么有的照片没有相机、镜头和参数？

  • 原图只在 iCloud 的照片，要在「设置」里打开「从 iCloud 读取拍摄参数」，下载原图后才能读到，每小时大约 1000 张。
  • 截图、从网上保存的图片和一些编辑后导出的照片，本身就没有保存这些信息。

可以不统计手机拍的照片吗？

可以。在「设置」里关掉「统计手机拍摄的照片」，就只统计相机拍的照片。

为什么地图上没有某些照片？

地图只显示保存了拍摄地点的照片。相机拍的照片通常要用相机厂商的 App 同步手机的位置，才会有拍摄地点。

更新照片要多久？

取决于照片的数量。iOS 26 起，你开始的更新切到别的 App 也会继续，进度显示在锁屏上。挑选配图用的评分在 App 打开时进行，夜里充电时也会给剩下的照片评分。

怎么删除数据？

在「设置」里点「重置数据」，或者删除 App。相册里的照片不会被删除。

Questions

Why does the app need access to my photos?

The statistics come from the shooting settings saved in your photos. They are read on your device only, never uploaded.

Can I allow access to some photos only?

Yes, Photo Walker then counts the photos you selected. The Statistics tab offers “Select More Photos” to add others.

Why do some photos have no camera, lens or settings?

  • Photos whose original is only in iCloud need “Read camera data from iCloud” turned on in Settings: the originals are downloaded to read them, about 1,000 photos an hour.
  • Screenshots, images saved from the web and some edited exports don't have this information.

Can I leave out the photos taken with my phone?

Yes. Turn off “Count Photos Taken with Phones” in Settings, only the photos of cameras are then counted.

Why are some photos missing from the map?

The map shows the photos that have a location saved in them. Photos from cameras usually get one from the app of the camera maker, which syncs the location of your phone.

How long does an update take?

It depends on the number of photos. From iOS 26, an update you start keeps going when you switch to another app, with its progress on the Lock Screen. The photos are scored to pick the ones to show while the app is open, and at night while the phone charges.

How do I delete the data?

Tap Reset Data in Settings, or delete the app. The photos in your library are never deleted.
